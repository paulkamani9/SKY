# Editing the SKY menu

Everything on the menu is edited at **sky-menu.vercel.app/studio** (bookmark it).
Changes reach the tables within a minute — there is nothing to publish twice, no
deploy, no developer.

The Studio opens on a list with three entries. They are ordered by how often you
will need them.

---

## Today

The two things that change week to week.

### Something has run out

**Today → Sold out right now** shows anything currently off the menu. To put a
thing back on, open it and press **Back on the menu**.

To take something off, open the item (see *Sections & items* below) and press
**Ran out — take off the menu**, the red button at the bottom of the item. It
disappears from the menu straight away and stays here, waiting, until you put it
back. Nothing is deleted, ever.

### The fruit has changed

**Today → Today's fruit selection** is one box. Type the fruits you have,
separated by commas:

```
Papaya, Mango, Shredded Coconut, Red Dragon Fruit, Bergamot
```

Press **Publish**. Guests reading Fruit Bowls and Smoothies now see exactly
those. This is the only place on the whole menu that names the fruit, so there
is nothing else to change when the season turns.

The French box below it is optional. Leave it empty and French guests see the
English list — better an English word than a blank space. Italian, German and
Russian sit under the **Other languages** tab, same rule.

## Sections & items

The everyday door. Tap a section — Gelato & Sorbets, Coffee — and you get its
items **in the order guests see them**.

### Change a price

Open the item. **Price** is near the top. Numbers only: type `250`, not
`Rs 250` — the "Rs" is added for you. Press **Publish**.

### Change the order

Drag the items by the handle on the left. That is the whole job; the menu
follows immediately. To reorder the sections themselves, go to
**Setup → Menu sections** and drag them there.

### Add an item

Press the **⋮** menu at the top of a section's item list and choose **New item
in this section**. It arrives already filed under that section, at the bottom.
Fill in:

- **Name** (English and French) — required.
- **Description** — the line a guest reads on the back of the tile.
- **Price** — or leave it empty and write a **price note** instead, like
  "Based on selection", for anything priced on the day.
- **Photo** — see below.

Press **Publish**. Drag it into place.

### Change a photo

Open the item, scroll to **Photo**, drop the new one in.

**The photo must be a cut-out on a transparent background — a PNG.** The menu
places the picture straight onto the blue page with no frame, so a photo with a
white background shows up as a white box. Square-ish, subject centred, at least
1200 pixels wide. If in doubt, send the photo to whoever shot the others rather
than uploading a snapshot.

### Fix a wording mistake

Open the item, edit the text, press **Publish**. The other languages are under
the **Other languages** tab — if you only fix the English, the other languages
keep showing their old wording, so fix both English and French at least.

## Setup

Things set once and rarely touched.

- **Restaurant settings** — the name, the line under it, the currency, the logo.
- **Menu sections** — a section's title, its intro line, its footnote, its
  banner photo, and whether it shows as a photo grid or a compact list. Drag to
  reorder the sections. **Hide this section** takes a whole section off the menu
  without deleting anything.
- **Groups inside sections** — the small headings inside a section, like
  "Premium Gelato". Rename one here and every item under it follows.

---

## Two things worth knowing

**Publish is the save button.** Until you press it, an edit is a draft that only
you can see. If you leave a document with an orange dot next to it, it is not on
the menu yet.

**Nothing you do here can break the page.** If Sanity is unreachable the menu
falls back to a copy built into the site. And every change is versioned — if
something goes wrong, the developer can put back any earlier version.
